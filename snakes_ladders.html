How to play
Everyone starts on square 1. Your first roll must be a 1 or 6 to move away from the start. Then roll to move. Rolling a 6 gives you another turn. Landing at the bottom of a ladder climbs you to its top and gives you another turn. Snakes slide you down. You need the exact roll to reach 100. First to 100 wins!
